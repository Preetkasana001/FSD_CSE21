    At ABES Engineering College, we ignite the flames of curiosity, knowledge, and innovation. Established over two decades ago under the esteemed Society for Educational Excellence, our institution has evolved into a dynamic center of learning nestled in the heart of Ghaziabad.

    Beyond engineering, our academic spectrum encompasses a diverse range of programs including B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA and MBA courses. Our core values of integrity, excellence and innovation continue to inspire generations of students.
